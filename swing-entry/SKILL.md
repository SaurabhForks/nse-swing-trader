# Swing Entry Skill

## Strategy Principles
1. **Stage 2 Trend Filter (Stan Weinstein & Mark Minervini)**:
   - Price must be above rising 50 EMA.
   - 50 EMA must be above 200 EMA.
   - No bottom-fishing or buying Stage 4 declining stocks.
2. **Relative Strength (RS Spread)**:
   - Stock 60-day momentum must exceed Nifty 50 60-day momentum.
   - Only trade market leaders outperforming the index.
3. **Volume Confirmation**:
   - Breakout volume must be $\ge 1.25\times$ the 20-day Volume SMA.
4. **Candle Quality**:
   - Close must be in the upper 45% of the daily range to confirm buyer dominance at the close.

## Setups
- **Minervini 50-Day Breakout**: Close breaks above highest high of the previous 50 trading days with volume expansion.
- **Supertrend Ignition**: Daily Supertrend (10, 3) flips bullish (+1) from bearish (-1) with RSI > 50 and RS outperformance.
